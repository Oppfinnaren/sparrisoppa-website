I'm a Software & Mechatronics engineer based in Stockholm, with a focus on creating refined products. I currently work at TMC as a software engineering consultant.

Before that I spent six years as a software engineer at Envac. I have a master's degree in mechanical engineering, specialising in mechatronics, from LTH, the Faculty of Engineering at Lund University.

Outside of work I build things of my own, from a robot that collects trash to an app for sorting notes and a text-based air traffic control game. This site collects them.
