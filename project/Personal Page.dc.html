Lantern

A local-first notes app that syncs when it can.

Year
2026
Role
Design, code
Stack
Rust, SQLite
Status
In progress

Lantern started as a way to keep notes on a train with no signal. Every note lives in a local database first, and changes merge in the background once a connection returns.

screenshot or diagram
Fig. 1, the sync model

Approach

Conflicts are resolved per paragraph rather than per document. This keeps edits from two devices readable and makes the history easy to inspect.

About

I'm a software engineer in Stockholm. I build tools for developers and small teams, and I care about software that is simple to run and simple to understand.

Before working independently I spent eight years at product companies, mostly on data infrastructure and internal platforms. This site collects what I've made and written since.
